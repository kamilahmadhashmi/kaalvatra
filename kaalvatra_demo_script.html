The Living Annals of Bharat — 2-Minute Video Demo Script
Live Demo: https://kamilahmadhashmi.github.io/kaalvatra/ Target Duration: 02:00 (270 Words) Speaker: Kamil Ahmad Hashmi
0:00 – 0:20 1. The Hook & The Problem
Screen: Open https://kamilahmadhashmi.github.io/kaalvatra/. Display the KAALVATRA masthead, paper grain styling, and scrolling lead story.
"Hello everyone! Modern news aggregation is broken. Centralized algorithms prioritize national sensationalism and celebrity clickbait, while critical grassroots journalism—state assembly debates, district court verdicts, and rural infrastructure—is completely buried under popups and ads. To solve this, we built KAALVATRA: The Living Annals of Bharat."
0:20 – 0:45 2. Interactive Bharat Atlas & Continuous Scroll
Screen: Click any state (e.g. Maharashtra or Kerala) on the map. Show the cinematic zoom, the watermark fade, and smoothly scroll down through the in-page state broadsheet.
"Rather than trapping users in cramped modal boxes, KAALVATRA features a custom-projected Interactive Bharat Atlas covering all 36 Indian States and Union Territories. When you click any state, the map transforms into a subtle watermark, and the state’s dedicated dispatches flow naturally into a single, continuous page. Readers can explore local bills, regional court rulings, and economic benchmarks with zero nested scrollbars."
0:45 – 1:15 3. Multi-Wire Dossiers & Direct Publisher Links
Screen: Click a story card. Point out the 'Story Graph Verification Rationale' badges. Scroll to 'Participating Wire Outlets' and click 'Read on The Hindu' or 'Read on Indian Express' to show the real article opening in a new tab.
"Every dispatch is an objective multi-wire consensus dossier. Our engine cross-references independent reports from competing newsrooms like The Hindu, The Indian Express, and NDTV. Readers get a verified consensus summary, transparent algorithmic verification signals, and direct links to the original publisher articles—with zero Google News redirects."
1:15 – 1:40 4. Architecture: GitHub Pages + AWS Serverless
Screen: Click 'The Machine (Pipeline)' in navigation. Demonstrate the Pipeline Sandbox and toggle to the AWS Architecture view.
"Our architecture unites modern cloud services with modern developer workflows: Our backend runs entirely on an AWS serverless architecture in Mumbai. Amazon EventBridge triggers hourly Lambda functions to ingest over 4,000 RSS feeds, scoring 460,000 candidate pairs using sparse TF-IDF and graph clustering stored in Amazon DynamoDB and served via Amazon API Gateway. Meanwhile, our frontend is hosted and continuously deployed via GitHub Actions directly onto GitHub Pages."
1:40 – 2:00 5. 6 Regional Languages, Zero Cost & Outro
Screen: Switch language dropdown to Hindi (हिन्दी) or Tamil (தமிழ்). Highlight the docked Gold/Silver/Nifty ticker, and conclude on the GitHub repository.
"For true accessibility across Bharat, the entire broadsheet instantly regionalizes across 6 major Indian languages with on-demand audio speech briefings, alongside a persistent live market ticker. Best of all, it runs with zero paid services at literally zero operating cost. Try it live right now at kamilahmadhashmi.github.io/kaalvatra. Thank you!"
